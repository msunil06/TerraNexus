🎓 Student Study Guide & Key Examination Concepts

  • Veto Mechanism: Any single negative vote by one of the P5 permanent members blocks any non-procedural Security Council resolution, even if supported by the other 14 members.
  • General Assembly Resolutions: Unlike Security Council resolutions (which can be legally binding under Chapter VII of the UN Charter), General Assembly resolutions carry moral and political weight but are generally non-binding.
  • UN Budget & Funding: Financed through assessed contributions based on each country's economic size and capacity to pay, with the United States historically assessed at 22% of the regular budget.
  • Six Official Languages: English, French, Spanish, Russian, Chinese, and Arabic are the official languages of the UN Secretariat and General Assembly.
