Accessibility Statement

TerraNexus is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all users.

1. Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1 AA)

We strive to conform to the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards across all interfaces.

2. Non-Visual & Text-Based Access to Geographic Data

All core geographic data, country capitals, currencies, areas, and demographics available on interactive map visualizations are also presented in structured HTML tables and accessible text lists, ensuring users utilizing screen readers or keyboards can explore all data without relying on map pointer gestures.

3. High Contrast & Reduced Motion Support

Our user interface includes both high-contrast Dark Cosmos and Light Atlas themes with contrast ratios exceeding 4.5:1. Micro-animations respect prefers-reduced-motion system settings.
